# Business Problem & Business Justification

## 1. Business Problem Statement

Library members experience severe operational delays in locating, reserving, borrowing, and returning literary resources due to inefficient manual tracking. Concurrently, administrative staff lack centralized real-time visibility, leading to unrecorded losses, reporting errors, and poor user satisfaction.

## 2. Core Library Operations

The Library Management Platform addresses the following academic library operations:

- Book Acquisition & Cataloging
- Membership Management
- Search & Circulation Workflow
- Financial Penalties
- Administrative Analytics

## 3. Manual Process Challenges

### Physical Book Searching

Manual searching through physical card catalogs causes delays in locating books.

### Paper-Based Checkout Records

Manual paper ledger entries make checkout processing slower and can introduce errors.

### Manual Overdue Tracking

Manually tracking overdue books makes it difficult to notify users and calculate penalties efficiently.

### Lost or Inaccurate Records

Human errors, lost records and missing inventory can affect the accuracy of library operations.

## 4. Automated Software Solutions

The proposed platform addresses these challenges through:

- Indexed database with multi-field search
- Automated barcode/QR scanner integration
- Cron-driven notifications and dynamic fine calculation
- Relational database with strict integrity constraints

## 5. Business Justification

The platform is intended to reduce operational delays, improve record accuracy, automate repetitive library processes, and provide better visibility for administrative staff.
